Duoke AI

CATEGORY AI Automation
ROLE Project Lead
YEAR 2026
STACK AI · Chat Automation

Duoke AI is an AI-powered customer service and automation system for Shopee stores, designed to improve seller response rates and streamline repetitive buyer interactions.

It supports automated replies, buyer reception, order and payment reminders, shipment notifications, and reusable quick responses — helping store handlers respond faster and manage inquiries more efficiently.

I led the integration of automated chat response across Shopee, TikTok Shop, and Lazada, and the system has maintained a 100% store chat response rate since rollout.
