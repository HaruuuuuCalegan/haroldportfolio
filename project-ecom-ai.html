ECOM AI

CATEGORY Systems & AI
ROLE AI Project Manager
YEAR 2026
STACK LLM · Prompt Engineering

Ecom AI is an internal AI consultant system built to centralize company knowledge and support more consistent e-commerce diagnostics and workflows across Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, and Meta Ads.

Store handlers and account managers can ask Ecom AI direct questions and get instant marketing insights, operational guidance, and scaling strategies pulled from a governed internal knowledge base — instead of digging through scattered documents or waiting on a senior teammate.

My role covered both the product side and the delivery side: structuring the knowledge base, writing and testing the prompt workflows, and coordinating the rollout across teams.
